# Code of Conduct

The SiteCTX project is intended to be a practical, vendor-neutral specification
effort. Participants are expected to help keep discussion technical, respectful,
and accessible to people building or operating websites and automated systems.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Assume good faith, while still asking for evidence and clear reasoning.
- Keep disagreement focused on the proposal, implementation, or tradeoff.
- Respect differing operational constraints across publishers, consumers, and
  tooling vendors.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when they are relevant to a proposal.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Misrepresenting SiteCTX as a certification, permission system, ranking
  promise, or legal guarantee.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the project maintainers through the repository issue
tracker or the contact channel listed by the current maintainers. Reports should
include enough context for maintainers to understand what happened and what
outcome is requested.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or
take other reasonable action to protect the project.
